Privacy Policy – Horizon App

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Horizon Solutions ("Horizon Solutions", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Horizon mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play under the package name ai.horizonsolutions.customerdemand.

In short: the App is a business tool for staff of organisations that use the Horizon demand planning platform. We collect only what is needed to sign you in and process your demand orders. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, we do not collect your location, and we do not sell your data.

2. About the App and who it is for

The App is the mobile front end for Horizon's demand planning system. Signed-in users can choose an organisation and role assigned to them, create customer demand orders by adding kits or individual items with quantities and dates, and view stock levels.

The App is intended only for business users: employees, staff and administrators of organisations that use the Horizon platform ("Client Organisations"), and their authorised customers. It is not intended for the general public or for children. Accounts cannot be created in the App. Each account is set up in the Horizon platform by or for the user's Client Organisation.

3. Information we collect

We collect only the information described below.

3.1 Account and profile information

This is provided by your Client Organisation when your account is set up, and used when you sign in:

  • Username and/or work email address
  • First name and last name
  • Password (sent securely to our server for authentication and never stored in readable form in the App)
  • The organisation(s) and role(s) assigned to you
  • Customer code and associated warehouse location (a business address, not your personal location)

3.2 Information you enter in the App

  • Demand orders, including kit or item codes, quantities and requested dates

3.3 Login session records

Our server records each sign-in session (for example, the time of sign-in and a session identifier) and may record related technical information, such as the IP address used to connect. We use this to keep your account secure and to operate the service.

3.4 Information we do NOT collect

  • No precise or approximate location (GPS)
  • No access to your photos, camera, files, contacts or microphone
  • No payment or financial information (the App has no payments)
  • No analytics, crash reporting, device identifiers or advertising ID
  • No cookies, advertising or cross-app tracking
  • No data collected in the background while the App is not in use

4. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Authenticate you and let you choose your organisation and role
  • Create, submit and show your demand orders and the relevant stock levels
  • Deliver orders to your Client Organisation as part of the service
  • Keep accounts and the service secure and prevent unauthorised access
  • Provide support and respond to your requests
  • Meet our legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

  • Your Client Organisation: orders you submit, together with your name, username and role, are made available to the organisation you submitted them for. This is how the service works. Your Client Organisation manages your account and may see your activity in the Horizon platform.
  • Hosting provider: our servers run on Amazon Web Services (AWS). AWS stores the data on our behalf and is not permitted to use it for its own purposes.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our users, Client Organisations or Horizon Solutions.
  • Business transfers: if Horizon Solutions is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred under the same protections described in this policy.

The App contains no third-party SDKs. All data travels only between the App and Horizon's own server at app.horizonsolutions.ai.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Horizon Solutions servers hosted by Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden (EU). This means your data is transferred outside India. We handle such transfers in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and protect the data as described in this policy wherever it is processed.

7. How we protect your data

  • All communication between the App and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS).
  • On your device, login tokens are stored in the operating system's encrypted secure storage.
  • Access to server data is restricted to authorised personnel who need it to operate and support the service.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will notify affected users, Client Organisations and authorities as required by law.

8. How long we keep data

  • We keep account information for as long as your account is active.
  • When an account is deleted, personal data (such as name and email) is deleted or anonymised within 30 days.
  • Backups containing that data are overwritten within 90 days.
  • Order records may be kept longer where your Client Organisation needs them for its business records, or where the law requires it.
  • Login session records are kept only as long as needed for security and operational purposes.

9. How to delete your account and data

Because your account is created and managed by your Client Organisation, you can ask for it and its data to be deleted in either of these ways:

  • Ask your organisation's Horizon administrator to delete your account, or
  • Email us at google@horizonsolutions.ai with the subject "Account deletion request – Horizon App". Include your username or work email and your organisation name.

We will verify the request and confirm when it is done. Data is deleted or anonymised as described in Section 8. Order records that your Client Organisation must keep for business or legal reasons may be retained in a form that is no longer linked to your personal profile where possible.

10. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Get information about the personal data we process about you
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update your personal data
  • Ask us to erase your personal data (see Section 9)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, noting this may mean you can no longer use the App
  • Have your grievances addressed (see Section 11)
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated

To use any of these rights, email google@horizonsolutions.ai. Where we process data on behalf of your Client Organisation, we may refer your request to it or act on its instructions.
